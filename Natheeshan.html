From drop-off to pickup

  1. Drop it off

    Bring the bike in any time we are open. No appointment needed for small jobs.

  2. We check it

    A mechanic looks over the whole bike and confirms the work on your ticket.

  3. You approve

    If we find something extra, we call you with the price. Nothing is done without a yes.

  4. Pick it up

    Ride away on the day your ticket says. You pay when you collect.
