# Sweet Crumbs image assets

Six sample cake images were created for this project using the built-in image generation tool, then encoded as WebP for web delivery. They are illustrative images, not photographs of a real bakery's products. The original composition is preserved. All final website image paths and the full prompts used are documented below. The SVG favicon and cupcake navigation icon were drawn directly in code.

## dist/assets/strawberry.webp

Use case: product-mockup. Asset: photorealistic bakery website photograph. A complete small round strawberry vanilla celebration cake with warm ivory buttercream, beautifully piped small pale-pink buttercream swirls around the upper edge, fresh whole and halved strawberries arranged on top, subtle fine blush pink icing ribbons on the sides. Cake on a sculptural ivory ceramic pedestal. A few strawberries and an ivory linen napkin near the base. Refined approachable home bakery, delicious real food, not overdecorated. Scene: warm pale cream studio wall and beige stone table, minimal props, sophisticated editorial food photography. Lighting: soft natural window light from the upper left with gentle realistic shadows, warm airy color grading, tactile cake texture, optical realism. Composition: portrait 4:5 composition. Leave air above the cake and fully show its pedestal. This is the principal landing page hero image.. Center the whole cake with generous space around it so it is safe to crop. The cake should fill about 65% of the frame, eye-level three-quarter perspective. Color palette: cream, cocoa brown, restrained blush pink. No people, no logos, no lettering, no text, no watermarks. This is an individual photograph, not a contact sheet or collage.
